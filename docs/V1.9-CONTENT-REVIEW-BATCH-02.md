# V1.9 Content Review — Batch 02

## Scope

Second-pass audit rules for the full question bank: scientific correctness, Grade 9 scope, item quality, and knowledge relationships.

## Mandatory checks

- One and only one defensible answer for single-answer questions.
- No explanation may contradict the key or the stem.
- Chemical names, formulas, states, conditions, observations, and equations must be internally consistent.
- Experimental questions must specify material/conditions whenever the conclusion depends on them.
- Difficulty and Bloom level must match the reasoning actually required.
- Knowledge links must resolve to canonical graph nodes.
- Questions marked `ready` must not depend on unstated assumptions that change the answer.
- Safety-related laboratory instructions must be age-appropriate and framed as supervised school-laboratory procedures.

## Gate policy

The automated integrity gate checks structural consistency and blocks publication when a known P0 item remains `ready`. Scientific judgment remains a human-review requirement for ambiguous experimental premises, curriculum interpretation, and wording where multiple defensible readings are possible.
