# Hubei Junior-High Chemistry Examination Material Audit 2024–2026

**Status:** C0.2 — Annual examination-material audit
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Scope:** Hubei Grade 9 chemistry assessment calibration for ChemLab-G9

## 1. Governing rule

Hubei's official unified-examination policy states that the provincial junior-high academic examination is based on the curriculum standard and does **not** formulate an examination syllabus. Therefore ChemLab must not invent or assume a separate annual “chemistry exam outline”.

The 2024–2026 annual layer is therefore treated as **calibration evidence**: verified official examination notices, requirements, sample materials, papers and answer materials where publicly released. These materials may calibrate assessment style and emphasis but may not override the 2022 curriculum standard or expand the textbook scope.

## 2. Verified official policy baseline

The Hubei Provincial Department of Education's unified-examination notice states that from 2024 Hubei implements unified junior-high examination for the specified subjects. Physics and Chemistry are a combined paper, with Chemistry allocated 50 points. The policy also states that examination content follows the curriculum standard and that high-school, competition, or private-training content must not be introduced merely because it appears in external materials.

Official policy interpretation further confirms that the unified examination follows the curriculum standard and does not establish an examination syllabus.

## 4. Search/audit result

The official Hubei policy sources located during this audit establish the governing framework and exam structure, but the search did not establish a verified annual document titled “湖北省中考化学考试说明/考纲” for each of 2024, 2025 and 2026. This is recorded as a source-audit result, not as evidence that no annual materials exist anywhere.

The next collection step must therefore use authoritative primary documents and official releases where available. Search-result summaries, private compilations, training materials, and unverified reposts are not admitted as S0/S1 authorities.

## 5. Assessment facts currently safe to encode

- From 2024, Hubei uses provincial unified examination for the specified junior-high subjects.
- Physics and Chemistry are combined; Chemistry has 50 points.
- The examination framework is curriculum-standard based.
- Hubei does not establish a separate examination syllabus under the verified policy interpretation.
- 2024–2026 are calibration years rather than separate curriculum/syllabus versions.

## 6. Evidence hierarchy

1. Hubei Provincial Department of Education official policy and policy interpretation.
2. Hubei Provincial Department of Education / Hubei Provincial Education Examinations Authority official annual notices or officially released materials.
3. Official examination papers and answer materials when officially released.
4. Only after the above: secondary sources may be used for discovery, never as the final authority.
