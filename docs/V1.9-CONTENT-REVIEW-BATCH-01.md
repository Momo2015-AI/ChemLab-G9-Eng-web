# V1.9 Content Review — Batch 01

Status: **REVIEWED — remediation required**

This batch records concrete issues found while moving from repository-level audit to item-level review. These findings are deliberately kept separate from the runtime question bank until each item is corrected and re-reviewed.

## Review criteria

Each item is checked for:

1. scientific correctness;
2. single-best-answer / answerability;
3. explanation consistency;
4. Grade 9 curriculum suitability;
5. cognitive-level alignment;
6. knowledge linkage.

## Findings

### Q-ACID-005 — P0 / answerability failure

Current stem: solution coexistence and colourless transparency.

Current options include both:

- `B. NaNO3, NaCl, NaOH`
- `C. KNO3, Na2SO4, NaCl`

Both groups are colourless and do not introduce an obvious precipitation, gas-generation or neutralisation conflict under the stated conditions. The current answer is `C`, while the explanation itself describes `B` as also non-reactive and colourless.

**Decision:** reject current item; rewrite the options so exactly one answer satisfies the stated conditions.

**Reason:** an assessment item cannot be published when its own explanation establishes more than one valid answer.

### Q-ACID-012 — P0 / stem–explanation mismatch

The stem asks for a pressure change that is “first increases then decreases”, but the explanation acknowledges that the proposed option does not cleanly satisfy the stated pattern and that another option may also show a pressure increase.

**Decision:** reject current item; replace it with a controlled experimental scenario where the pressure variable, apparatus, temperature and gas-generation/consumption process are explicitly specified.

**Reason:** the current item tests interpretation of an underspecified physical system rather than the intended chemistry concept.

### Q-ACID-001 — P1 / terminology precision

The explanation says `HCl` is “氯化氢的水溶液，属于酸”. Strictly, HCl denotes hydrogen chloride; its aqueous solution is hydrochloric acid / 盐酸. In Grade 9 material this distinction should be made explicit to avoid conflating a substance formula with the solution name.

**Decision:** rewrite the stem and explanation using the curriculum convention consistently.

## Content-governance rule introduced by this batch

A question with any of the following conditions is **not publishable**:

- more than one defensible answer;
- no defensible answer;
- explanation contradicts the answer;
- stem omits a condition needed to determine the answer;
- scientific terminology is materially ambiguous;
- the item requires knowledge outside the declared Grade 9 scope.
