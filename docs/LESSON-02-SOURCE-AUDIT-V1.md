## Source verification

People's Education Press (PEP) official catalogue confirms that Grade 9 Chemistry Upper Book places Unit 1 `走进化学世界` before Unit 2 and that Topic 2 is `化学是一门以实验为基础的科学`. This establishes the curriculum identity and sequence.

Until then, this lesson may use only conservative, textbook-aligned instructional claims.

### Wuhan examination calibration
Wuhan examination materials are calibration evidence only. They may inform experimental reasoning, evidence interpretation, variable control and item demand, but no copyrighted question wording is copied into this lesson.

## Scope decision
Lesson 02 follows PEP Unit 1 Topic 2 and focuses on:
- scientific inquiry as an evidence-based process;
- observation versus explanation/conclusion;
- fair comparison and variable control;
- recording and interpreting evidence;
- reflection when evidence conflicts with a prediction.

The lesson does NOT yet claim mastery of advanced experimental-design terminology beyond the Grade 9 boundary.

## Safety decision
Learner-facing activities use teacher-controlled demonstrations, supplied observations or digital observation records. Students are not asked to smell, taste or handle unknown substances.
