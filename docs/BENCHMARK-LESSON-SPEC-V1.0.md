# ChemLab-G9 Benchmark Lesson Specification V1.0

A benchmark lesson is the quality gate before scaling content production across the curriculum.

## Required sections

1. Learning context
2. Learning objectives
3. Prerequisite check
4. Phenomenon / problem context
5. Guided observation or experiment
6. Evidence record
7. Macro–micro–symbolic explanation
8. Core vocabulary and chemical notation
9. Worked example(s)
10. Guided practice
11. Independent check
12. Misconception diagnosis
13. Remediation
14. Transfer task
15. Exit ticket
16. Source references

## Quality requirements

- Every factual claim must trace to an approved source or be a transparent pedagogical inference.
- Experimental descriptions must be scientifically accurate and age-appropriate.
- Chemical equations and quantitative calculations must be independently checked.
- No lesson may depend on the retired 320-question dataset.
- Activities must be feasible for a normal Grade 9 learner; unsafe procedures are excluded from student-facing instructions.
- The lesson must distinguish observation from explanation and explanation from inference.
- Assessment must measure the stated objective rather than reward memorized wording.

## Audit record

Each benchmark lesson must carry a machine-readable audit record containing:

- source status
- science status
- curriculum status
- age-appropriateness status
- experiment status
- assessment status
- knowledge-linkage status
- reviewer notes

Only `READY` lessons may be promoted into the canonical production content set.
