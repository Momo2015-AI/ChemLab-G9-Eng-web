## Resolutions

### q-acid-012 — P0 resolved in runtime content

The legacy pressure-trend item was internally inconsistent and under-specified. It is now quarantined by ID and replaced by a fully specified conceptual item covering a sealed rigid vessel, approximately constant temperature, constant gas volume, and increasing CO2 amount.

The replacement tests the distinction between decreasing gas-generation rate and decreasing pressure, avoiding the original ambiguous premise.

### q-acid-001 — P1 terminology defect resolved in runtime content

The replacement explicitly classifies HCl as hydrogen chloride and separately explains that hydrochloric acid is the aqueous solution of hydrogen chloride. The original explanation's incorrect equivalence has been removed from the effective runtime question set.

### q-acid-004 — P1 safety wording resolved in runtime content

The replacement removes the fixed-concentration neutralization instruction. It assesses immediate water flushing, reporting to the teacher, and following the approved laboratory emergency procedure. It does not ask students to improvise chemical treatment.

### q-acid-011 — P1 model wording resolved in runtime content

The replacement explicitly labels Fe2O3 as a simplified junior-high model for rust composition and states that real rust is compositionally more complex.

### q-acid-005 — known ambiguity also resolved

A previously quarantined Day 01 item had more than one plausible correct option. It is now replaced by a unique coexistence item with mutually exclusive failure reasons for the distractors.
