## Blocking issues

### P1 — Independent scientific review

The lesson contains claims about common acids, volatility, absorbency/corrosivity, rust composition, reaction equations, and observation/interpretation. These require independent chemistry review before publication.

The rust experiment should present Fe2O3 as a simplified junior-high model for rust composition, not imply that real rust is a pure Fe2O3 substance.

### P1 — Safety boundary

Student-facing content must teach safety principles without becoming a procedural guide for hazardous chemical handling. The Day 01 diagnostic items were revised to test recognition of heat-release/splash risk and adherence to approved laboratory supervision rather than teach operational handling steps.

The original safety-sensitive production question `q-acid-004` remains excluded pending independent review/replacement.

### P1 — Existing production question

`q-acid-001` remains excluded because its HCl/hydrochloric-acid terminology requires correction.

### P1 — Runtime verification

All `q-acid-dx-*` and `q-acid-rx-*` identifiers must be resolved by the actual runtime/content loader and validated by automated integrity checks.

### P1 — Grade-9 pedagogical review

Review whether terminology density, prerequisite assumptions, and the amount of acid-safety content are appropriate for the intended Grade-9 learner.
