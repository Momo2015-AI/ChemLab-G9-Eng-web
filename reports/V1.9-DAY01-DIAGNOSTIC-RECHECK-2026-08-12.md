## Design rule

A recheck is not a copy of the diagnostic question. It must use a different surface form or context while testing the same underlying misconception.

## Quality notes

- The HCl recheck uses `HCl(aq)` notation rather than repeating the original naming question.
- The acid–metal recheck uses a comparison of metals rather than repeating the universal-statement prompt.
- The observation recheck asks the learner to identify a direct observation in a different experimental context.
- The dilution recheck tests transfer of the safety principle rather than simply repeating the original instruction.

## Safety boundary

Safety-sensitive content remains review-only until independently verified against the project's approved school-laboratory safety guidance. The recheck intentionally avoids asking the learner to perform an unsafe action.
