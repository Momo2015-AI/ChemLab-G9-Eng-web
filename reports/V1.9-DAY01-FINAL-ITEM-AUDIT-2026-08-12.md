# V1.9 Day 01 Final Item Audit — 2026-08-12

## Scope

Deep review of the production question bank items that are relevant to Day 01 (`常见的酸`) and adjacent acid content.

## Findings

### P0 — q-acid-012 has an internally inconsistent answer key

The item asks about a pressure change described as “first increases then decreases”. Its answer key and explanation do not agree: the key selects B, while the explanation states that A and B may both show pressure increase/stabilization and does not establish a unique answer. The question also lacks an explicit apparatus and gas-volume/temperature assumption.

Decision: **do not publish**. Rework or replace the item with a fully specified experimental system.

### P1 — q-acid-001 terminology/explanation defect

The current explanation incorrectly states that HCl is “hydrogen chloride's aqueous solution”. HCl denotes hydrogen chloride; hydrochloric acid denotes an aqueous solution of hydrogen chloride. The item should explicitly distinguish the substance formula from the solution name.

Decision: keep the item out of the Day 01 benchmark until corrected and independently reviewed.

### P1 — q-acid-004 safety item

The legacy item gives a fixed concentration for a post-exposure neutralizing solution. This is not appropriate as a generic student-facing safety instruction. The benchmark should teach immediate flushing and following the approved laboratory emergency procedure rather than asking students to improvise a chemical neutralization treatment.

Decision: quarantine/rewrite; independent safety review required before publication.

### P1 — q-acid-011 rust model wording

The item should frame Fe2O3 as a simplified junior-high model for rust composition rather than state that real rust is simply Fe2O3.

Decision: retain only with explicit model wording.

## Positive findings

- q-acid-002 directly assesses volatility of concentrated hydrochloric acid.
- q-acid-003 addresses the dilution safety principle.
- q-acid-006 targets acid-property misconceptions.
- q-acid-007 targets acid–metal reaction conditions.
- q-acid-008/009/011 connect equations to acid properties and rust removal.

## Release decision

Day 01 remains **BLOCKED / NOT READY**.

The item gate cannot pass until P0 is zero and all P1 items included in the lesson are corrected or replaced.
