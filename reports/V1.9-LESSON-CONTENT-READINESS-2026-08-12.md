# V1.9 Lesson Content Readiness Audit — 2026-08-12

## Executive result

The lesson set is **NOT publication-ready**.

The audit establishes a content-readiness baseline and confirms that the lesson architecture must not be treated as evidence of completed teaching content.

## Scope

Target: `modules/lessons/day-*.json`

Expected course set: 36 lessons.

## Audit rules

A lesson is classified as `template` when its primary teaching sections contain generic placeholder patterns or text that could be reused for another lesson without changing its instructional meaning.

A lesson is classified as `ready` only when it passes the V1.9 Content Standard and Review Protocol, including curriculum, scientific, Grade-9 suitability, content-quality, knowledge-link, and learning-flow requirements.

## Findings

The inspected lesson set contains the known generic section pattern:

- Learning objectives: generic "master the core concepts and properties" wording.
- New knowledge: generic "observe, experiment and discuss the chemical principles" wording.
- Worked example: generic "combine typical examples" wording.
- Practice: generic "complete 5 in-class exercises" wording.

These are structural placeholders, not topic-specific teaching content.

The repository also contains a legacy `day01.json` alongside `day-01.json`. It must remain outside the canonical lesson loader until its usage is verified and the duplicate is safely removed.

## Baseline decision

The project must treat the affected lesson set as `rewrite-required`, not `ready`.

The exact numerical coverage count must be generated by the automated scanner and retained as machine-readable output. This report intentionally does not infer counts from filenames alone.

## Priority

### P0

- Replace placeholder teaching sections with real chemistry instruction.
- Prevent placeholder lessons from being marked `ready`.
- Complete Day 01 benchmark lesson.

### P1

- Add misconception records and diagnostic links.
- Link lessons to real question IDs.
- Link experiments to misconceptions and questions.
- Remove or archive legacy duplicate lesson artifacts after loader verification.

## Next step

Build Day 01 as the benchmark lesson, run scientific and Grade-9 suitability review, then use the approved benchmark to rewrite the remaining lessons.
